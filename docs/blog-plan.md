# Blog Content Plan — Australian Electric Motor Co

*Built from the keywords that didn't map to an existing product/category/brand page. Filtered
to Informational + Question intent only, on-niche (dropped street-motorcycle/bicycle/Harley/BMX
contamination that would never convert here), clustered by topic, ranked by a volume ÷ (KD+5)
composite score so easy, real demand beats merely-large, hard demand. 27 clusters cleared the ≥2-keyword
bar — top 25 below, implemented now; the rest banked at the bottom.*

**Re-verified against the v3 broad-scope (unfiltered, 15,920-keyword) dataset.** The unmatched pool
grew from 1,661 to 12,358 keywords, but re-clustering it turned up the *same* top clusters at
essentially identical volumes — every one of the 25 posts below still leads its cluster with the same
number. That's expected, not a bug: the old volume-50 floor barely touched genuine Informational/
Question-intent keywords (the skill's carve-out already let Commercial/Transactional down to Vol 20
through), so almost nothing in this specific intent slice was sitting below the old floor. What the
broad scope *did* add was 22 more thin clusters (all under ~90 combined volume, KD 12–34) in the tail
below the old #27 cutoff — folded into "Banked for future content" below, not worth displacing anything
in the top 25.

**Every brief includes** (per keyword-engine spec, applied uniformly rather than repeated 25 times
below): meta description 150–160 chars with the primary keyword; H2/H3 outline opening with a
150-word "definition hook" answering the primary keyword directly (featured-snippet bait); 3–5 FAQ
questions pulled from the cluster's own question-phrased keywords + Speakable schema on the single
best answer; Article schema (+ FAQPage where the post has a FAQ block); 2–4 images (product shots for
Bottom-funnel posts, diagram/lifestyle for Top-funnel); internal links to the 1–3 most relevant
product/category pages from `keyword-map.md`, and a link back to its pillar post where one exists;
CTA scaled to funnel position (Bottom → "Shop [category]", Middle → "Compare models" / newsletter,
Top → read the pillar or browse the catalogue).
